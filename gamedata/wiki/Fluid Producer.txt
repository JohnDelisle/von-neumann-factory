== Overview ==

The Fluid Producer produces the throughput of 24/19.2/16 [[Fluid Launcher]]s of any fluid color.

The Fluid Producer is unlocked with cheat commands, see [[Sandbox]].

After placing the Fluid Producer, select it and select the desired color from the dropdown menu.
