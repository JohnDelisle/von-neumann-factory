= Fluids =
== Overview ==

Fluids are mined from Fluid Asteroids using [[Asteroid Pump]]s.  They are transported with [[Pipe]]s or [[Space Pipe]]s or fluid wagons on [[Trains]].  Machines that use fluids include the [[Painter]] and the [[Crystal Generator]].  The Fluid Asteroids on the map provide only a few fluid colors.  Other colors are obtained by [[Color Mixer|mixing]] two colors together.

== Fluid Colors ==

There are nine fluid colors.

Note that the displayed color depends on the [[Color Modes|Color Mode]].  See the color mode [[Color Modes#Color Comparison | comparison table]] for details.
