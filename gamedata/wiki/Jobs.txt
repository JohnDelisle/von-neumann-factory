= Jobs =
== Overview ==

Jobs are sequences of goals where each step builds on the prior step, with increasing complexity. Completing jobs awards <span class="gl">Research Points</span>.

Each Classic mode [[Scenario]] has its own set Jobs.

== Job Lists ==

The number of shapes required to complete each job, as well as the rewards for completing each job vary depending on the Goal Multiplier, the Copy/Paste Cost, and the Platform Limit selected through the [[Difficulty]] setting. The numbers provided in the following sections are for each of these settings at 100%. If Copy/Paste is free and/or the Platform Limit is unlimited, the respective rewards will not be shown or awarded.
