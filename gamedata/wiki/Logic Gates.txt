== Overview ==

Logic Gates output a signal dependent on whether their inputs are <code>truthy</code> or not. All Logic Gates, aside from the [[Transistor Gate]] will always output either a <code>1</code> or a <code>0</code>.

== Comparison Gate ==

The Comparison Gate compares the signals from its inputs according to the configured compare mode. It can be configured in the tooltip to compare by <code>Equal</code>, <code>Greater</code>, <code>GreaterEqual</code>, <code>Less</code>, <code>LessEqual</code>, or <code>NotEqual</code>.

For <code>Equal</code> and <code>NotEqual</code>, it will work on all signal types, compare if they are strictly equal / not equal, and output <code>0</code> or <code>1</code> accordingly. For the other modes, if any of the signals are not integers, it will always output <code>0</code>, otherwise it will compare according to the selected mode and output <code>0</code> or <code>1</code>.

== Transistor Gate ==

The Transistor Gate, passes any signal that is received from the rear <u>'''if'''</u> the input from the side is <code>truthy</code>. The Transistor Gate is unique in that it is the only logic gate capable of outputting any type of signal, not just <code>0</code> or <code>1</code>.

When the Transistor Gate's side input is not <code>truthy</code>, the output is <code>null</code> and not <code>0</code> like other logic gates.
