= Milestones =
== Overview ==

Milestones are the primary goals in Shapez 2 and require large numbers of shapes to complete. Completing Milestones unlocks new machines, new [[Upgrades]], and new [[Jobs]].

Each [[Scenario]] has its own set of Milestones.

== Milestone Lists ==

Milestones are divided into their respective scenarios.

Milestone shapes build on each other, indicated by arrows between the shapes.

The number of shapes required to complete each milestone, as well as the rewards for completing each milestone vary depending on the Goal Multiplier, the Copy/Paste Cost, and the Platform Limit selected through the [[Difficulty]] setting. The numbers provided in the following sections are for each of these settings at 100%. If Copy/Paste is free and/or the Platform Limit is unlimited, the respective rewards will not be shown or awarded.
