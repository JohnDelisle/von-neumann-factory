== Overview ==

The Painter takes shapes on the belt input and fluid through any of the 3 pipe inputs and paints the top layer of the shape the color of the fluid provided.

== Specifics ==

If the color of the fluid is changed, the fluid in the painter will drain and be replaced with the new color.

Painter pipe ports are bidirectional.  Fluid can pass through the painter to another fluid machine such as [[Pipe]], another Painter, or to any other machine with a fluid input.

The painter will only change the color of regular [[Shapes#Part Types | shapes]] (types C, R, S, W, F, G, H). Refined shapes (types X and Y), [[Pins]] and [[Crystals]] will go through unchanged.
