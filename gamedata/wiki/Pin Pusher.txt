{{Shape Processing}}

== Overview ==

The Pin Pusher pushes [[Pins]] under shapes received as input and outputs the result.

== Specific Process ==

Firstly, pins are created under every non-empty shape part of the shape's bottom layer.

Spoiler warning : the next step is a solution for how to get pins without anything on top of them in a shape, you might prefer figuring out the solution by yourself first.

{{spoiler|Then, any layers above the limit (5 if insane [[Scenario]], 4 otherwise) get discarded. For [[Crystals]] in the layer that gets discarded, any that was directly above another crystal will make it shatter along with all other connected crystals.}}

Finally, the [[Shape Gravity Rules]] are applied to the resulting shape.
