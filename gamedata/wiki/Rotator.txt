== Rotator ==

The Rotator rotates shapes it receives one shape part clockwise and outputs the result. In regular [[Scenarios]], this results in a 90 degrees rotation and in a 60 degrees rotation for the hexagonal scenario.

== Reverse Rotator ==

The Reverse Rotator rotates shapes it receives one shape part counterclockwise and outputs the result. In regular [[Scenarios]], this results in a 90 degrees rotation and in a 60 degrees rotation for the hexagonal scenario.

== 180° Rotator ==

The 180° Rotator rotates shapes it receives 180 degrees and outputs the result.

== Additional Notes ==

The rotation of a shape does not matter for the [[Vortex]].

Different rotations will result in different results from other machines such as the [[Half Destroyer]], [[Cutter#Cutter | Cutter]], [[Swapper]], [[Stacker]]s, and wire machines including the [[Flow Control]] machines and the [[Shape Analyzer]].

The 'No Alternate Rotators' [[Game Modes#Challenges|challenge]] limits the rotators available to only the Rotator.
