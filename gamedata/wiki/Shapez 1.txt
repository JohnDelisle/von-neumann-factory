= Shapez 1 =
'''Shapez''', previously known as '''shapez.io''', is the first game in the Shapez franchise. For clarity, this wiki (and other Shapez 2 community spaces as well) refers to it as '''Shapez 1''', to differentiate it from '''Shapez 2'''.

If Shapez 1 is owned on Steam, its soundtrack will play in the background in-game, if enabled in the audio settings.
