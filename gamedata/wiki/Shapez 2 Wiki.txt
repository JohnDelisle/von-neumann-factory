<div {{#var:cell-style}}><span class="shapez-header">About</span>
Successor to the popular Shapez game, Shapez 2 allows you to build huge 3D factories in space.  Mine raw shapes from asteroids, assemble and paint them using factories you build, and deliver them to the giant Vortex.  You can see how every machine works in a stress-free environment.
