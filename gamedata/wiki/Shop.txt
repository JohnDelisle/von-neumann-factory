= Shop =
== Overview ==

Upgrades and unlockable items may be purchased from the Shop with <span class="gl">Research Points</span>. The shop has six categories: [[#Upgrades|Upgrades]], [[#Machines|Machines]], [[#Platforms|Platforms]], [[#Trains|Trains]], [[#Wires|Wires]] and [[#Decorations|Decorations]].

Note: The Milestone Required is the Milestone for the 'Classic-Regular' [[Scenario]].

== Upgrades ==

Upgrades speed up shape production and delivery.

Increases the speed of all Machines and Platforms - affects both processing and transport speed.

When shapes are delivered to the [[Vortex]] either via [[Belt]]s or [[Trains]], the amount of shapes will be multiplied by the current multiplier. Due to its high costs, the Shape Multiplier is end-game and emulates an infinite upgrade similar to [[Shapez 1]].

Note: The 'Operator Level' row in the table above indicates which [[Operator Level]] is required to have enough Research Points to buy the current level, assuming a save in the Normal scenario with all milestones and jobs completed, all unlockable and linear upgrades bought, including the [[Trains#Cosmetic Rails|Trains - Roller Coaster]] upgrade, but excluding the Shape Multiplier.
