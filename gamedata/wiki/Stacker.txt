{{Shape Processing}}

== Overview ==

The Stacker stacks the shapes it receives in the top input on top of the shapes in the bottom input and outputs the result.

== Stacking process summary ==

The top shape will fall as far down as possible, this means that two full shapes will end up on each other ({{ShapeRender|RuRuRuRu|24}} + {{ShapeRender|CuCuCuCu|24}} = {{ShapeRender|RuRuRuRu:CuCuCuCu|24}}) but two opposite halves will combine into a single full layer ({{ShapeRender|RuRu----|24}} + {{ShapeRender|----CuCu|24}} = {{ShapeRender|RuRuCuCu|24}}).

Additionally, any [[Crystal]] that falls will shatter.

== Specifics ==

Firstly, the top shape is put above the bottom shape with one empty layer in between them.

Then, the [[Shape Gravity Rules]] are applied to the resulting shape.

Spoiler warning : the next step is a solution for how to get pins without anything on top of them in a shape, you might prefer figuring out the solution by yourself first.

{{spoiler|Finally, any layers above the limit (5 if insane scenario, 4 otherwise) get discarded.}}
