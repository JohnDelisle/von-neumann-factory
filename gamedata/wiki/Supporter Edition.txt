= Supporter Edition =
The Supporter Edition DLC of Shapez 2 adds the purely cosmetic [[Trains#Cosmetic Rails|Rail Twister, Rail Loop, Long Rail Up, and Long Rail Down]], doubles the amount of soundtrack that can play in the background in-game, and allows to access the full soundtrack on Steam.
